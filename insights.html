Quantity Audit

How Does a Quantity Audit Prevent Overbilling?

Contractor billing often diverges from actual floor executions due to unverified theoretical estimates, unapplied architectural deductions (e.g. wall openings, duct cutouts), and unapproved variation rates.

Core Mechanism: Independent Joint Measurement Sheets (JMS) signed on site, strict enforcement of IS measurement standards, and mathematical reconciliation against approved BOQ rates before commercial signoff.

Asset Verification

What Should a Fixed Asset Verification Report Contain?

An auditable asset verification dossier must go beyond an Excel list. It requires clear evidence that satisfy external statutory auditors and company directors.

Key Elements: Unique QR/barcode master register, room-level location mapping, custodian acknowledgments, geotagged condition photos, missing (ghost) asset schedules, and variance write-off calculations.

Asset Controls

FAR vs. Physical Register: Why Differences Occur

Why do physical assets on site stop matching balance sheet records? Common root causes include unrecorded inter-branch transfers, scrap disposals, and lack of physical tag discipline.

Solution: Two-way reconciliation protocol identifying matched assets, unrecorded physical items, and ghost ledger items with recommended accounting treatment.

Asset Tagging

QR vs. Barcode Tags: Which Should Businesses Use?

Evaluating asset tagging media based on operational environment, scanning hardware requirements, and data density.

Recommendation: 2D QR codes on tamper-evident polyester or anodized aluminum enable smartphone camera scanning and encode secure URLs, whereas 1D barcodes require dedicated laser guns.

Materials

What Is Material Reconciliation on Site?

How structured inward registers, authorized issue slips, and theoretical consumption ratios uncover site wastage, contractor pilferage, and unaccounted losses.

The Formula: Received Inward = Physically Installed Output + Legitimate Contract Wastage + Unconsumed Store Balance. Any gap is commercially recovered.

Procurement

What Belongs in a Commercial Comparison Statement?

Why the lowest headline bid is rarely the lowest actual cost. Structuring a robust Commercial Comparison Statement (CCS) to normalize competing vendor bids.

Critical Fields: Scope exclusion adjustments, freight terms, payment retention, warranty liability, and total cost of ownership across lifecycle.

Vendor Management

How Should Vendors Be Prequalified?

Essential screening criteria to prevent supplier failure before tenders are issued. A 6-pillar prequalification framework.

Filters: Factory tooling capacity, banking solvency, statutory GST/PF compliance, technical leadership, committed order book, and past client audits.

Workforce

When Does a Project Need Project Manpower?

Balancing permanent organizational headcount against peak-phase site resourcing demands.

Decision Matrix: Ideal during temporary 3-9 month execution peaks, specialized QA/QC audits, multi-city rollout surges, and remote site supervision.

Execution Methodology

How Do Multi-Location Asset Audits Work?

Managing synchronized physical verification sweeps across 20 to 100+ distributed retail or corporate branches concurrently.

Operational Playbook: Standardized taxonomy, centralized tag printing, decentralized off-hours audit teams, and cloud-synced daily exception logs.
