ABOUT WAKEFIELD CC LLP

Practical Project Expertise. Built Around Client Control.

Wakefield CC LLP is an integrated project consultancy providing professional project management, commercial assurance, asset, procurement, material-management and project workforce solutions.

WHO WE ARE

More Than a Conventional PMC.

Traditional project-management consultancy focuses primarily on project planning and high-level delivery schedules. Wakefield's model extends significantly further into physical reality.

We operate as an integrated project control and execution partner. We bridge the gap between executive strategy and on-site reality by combining professional management methodologies with direct, physical verification at project sites.

Our coordinated platform brings together eight key operational capabilities to ensure clients maintain total visibility over their capital investments:

OUR CODE

Six Operating Principles Built for Site Reality.

Every assignment undertaken by Wakefield teams is governed by six strict professional standards.

01 / PRINCIPLE

Accuracy

Measure and verify before reporting. We do not rely on contractor claims or secondary spreadsheets. Physical verification is the prerequisite to commercial reporting.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Accountability

Take ownership of agreed responsibilities. We act as accountable project partners, standing behind our measurements, audits, and coordination milestones.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Transparency

Communicate issues and information clearly. Bad news does not improve with age. We surface discrepancies, variances, and site risks early so clients can act with certainty.

04 / PRINCIPLE

Practicality

Develop solutions suited to actual site conditions. Project controls must function in active work environments, not just in theoretical manuals.

05 / PRINCIPLE

Documentation

Turn project information into usable management records. We deliver structured, auditable evidence and clear comparison statements ready for executive review.

06 / PRINCIPLE

Partnership

Work as an extension of the client's project team. We collaborate closely with client leadership, consultants, and contractors to solve problems collaboratively.
