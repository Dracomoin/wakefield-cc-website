Luxury Retail

Multi-Location Boutique Asset Verification

Location: Mumbai & New Delhi
Requirement: Physical verification, metallic QR code tagging, and Fixed Asset Register (FAR) reconciliation for high-value retail stores.
Challenge: Active luxury store operations with zero tolerance for visual clutter, requiring complete off-hours execution with unresolved legacy asset movements.
Wakefield Scope: Overnight physical store sweeps, discreet barcode/QR tagging, digital photographic logging, and two-way ledger reconciliation against financial books.
Methodology: Systematic room-by-room floor sweeps, serialized QR scanning, asset description standardization, and automated variance cross-matching.
Deliverables & Outcome: Clean QR-tagged master asset register, identified ghost assets for accounting write-off, and 100% physically reconciled store balance sheets.
Commercial Fit-Out

Corporate Workplace Quantity Assurance

Location: Hyderabad, Telangana
Requirement: Independent contractor bill verification, joint physical measurements, and variance analysis for 45,000 sq.ft office fit-out.
Challenge: Multiple contractor variation claims, accelerated milestone schedules, and substantial divergence between submitted measurement sheets and site installations.
Wakefield Scope: On-site dimensional checking across civil, finishes, and MEP installations; structural deduction audits; contract rate analysis.
Methodology: Joint Measurement Sheets (JMS) executed jointly with contractor site engineers, line-by-line drawing cross-checks, and exception statements.
Deliverables & Outcome: Certified contractor payment recommendation, itemized disallowed overclaims, and fully reconciled final closeout certification.
Hospitality

Resort Material & Site Coordination

Location: Dandeli, Karnataka
Requirement: Inward material tracking, site store security, supplier delivery synchronization, and FF&E inventory management for river resort development.
Challenge: Remote eco-sensitive location prone to supply chain delays, severe monsoon weather risks, and material wastage.
Wakefield Scope: Establishment of gate inward registers, physical stock verification, authorized indent-based issues, and consumption monitoring.
Methodology: Theoretical vs. actual consumption auditing against engineering drawings, and real-time vendor delivery expediting.
Deliverables & Outcome: Zero project stoppages caused by material stockouts, tight wastage control within allowable margins, and reconciled handover inventory.
