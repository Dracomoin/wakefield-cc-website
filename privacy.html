Privacy Policy

How Wakefield CC LLP collects, manages, and protects business enquiry information and project documentation.

1. Information We Collect

Wakefield CC LLP collects information provided voluntarily when you submit an enquiry, request a proposal, download our capability statement, or contact us. This includes: name, designation, company name, corporate email address, telephone number, project location, scope descriptions, and attached RFQ or architectural documents.

2. Use of Information

Information collected is strictly utilized to:

  • Evaluate project requirements and formulate commercial proposals.
  • Coordinate technical discussions with designated project managers.
  • Conduct conflict-of-interest checks before entering advisory agreements.
  • Maintain statutory corporate records and communicate official service updates.

3. Project Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Wakefield treats all submitted engineering drawings, tender BOQs, financial estimates, and project specifications as proprietary and confidential. We never sell, lease, or monetize client data. Technical documents are accessible solely to authorized engineering personnel under standard professional non-disclosure obligations.

4. Analytics & Cookies

Our website uses minimal technical cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to evaluate site responsiveness, browser compatibility, and traffic distribution. No personally identifiable tracking data is transferred to third-party advertising networks. See our Cookie Policy for details.

5. Data Retention & Security

Project records and enquiry communications are retained in secure administrative systems for duration necessary to fulfill commercial and statutory compliance under Indian law.
