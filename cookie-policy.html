Cookie Policy

Information on technical cookies and device storage utilized on the Wakefield CC LLP website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They assist in technical site performance, navigation persistence, and responsive presentation.

2. How Wakefield Uses Cookies

Wakefield CC LLP utilizes strictly essential technical cookies necessary for website operation, such as preserving user modal preferences and maintaining navigation state across screen sizes. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies or third-party behavioral advertising beacons.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive functions, such as the capability statement modal dialog.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our cookie practices, please contact info@wakefieldcc.com.
