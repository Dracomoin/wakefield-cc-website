PROJECT MANAGEMENT CAPABILITY

Keep Your Project Moving. Keep Management in Control.

Wakefield provides structured project-management support from planning and mobilization through execution, monitoring, handover and closure.

01 / CLIENT PROBLEM

Why Conventional Projects Drift

Projects involve multiple stakeholders, changing priorities, strict deadlines, commercial requirements and complex site-level dependencies. Without structured coordination, small misalignments between consultants, contractors, and suppliers rapidly escalate into substantial schedule delays, contractual disputes, unverified claims, and cost overruns.

Management teams frequently discover slippages only after milestones are missed, because conventional reporting focuses on administrative paperwork rather than the physical reality on the site floor.

02 / WAKEFIELD SOLUTION

Integrated Project Control & On-Site Coordination

Wakefield acts as a dedicated project-control and coordination partner focused on maintaining absolute visibility across schedule, stakeholders, documentation, cost and site activity.

We bridge executive decision-makers and site operations. Our project managers establish clear operational baselines, track physical progress against engineering drawings, actively coordinate daily contractor dependencies, and turn raw site data into clear, auditable management information.

04 / METHODOLOGY

How We Work: Four Controlled Phases

Phase 1: Mobilization & PEP Formulation Establish Project Execution Plan (PEP), define master control schedules, responsibility assignment matrices (RACI), and communication protocols.
Phase 2: Execution & Site Controls Deploy on-site project coordinators, conduct joint progress measurements, hold structured weekly coordination sessions, and track critical paths.
Phase 3: Variance & Risk Interventions Active mitigation of schedule deviations, commercial change-order auditing, and coordination of material deliveries to prevent floor stoppage.
Phase 4: Commissioning & Handover Comprehensive physical snagging, contractor de-mobilization verification, operational document consolidation, and formal asset handover.
