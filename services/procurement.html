01 / CLIENT PROBLEM

The Trap of the Lowest-Price Bid

Procurement decisions in complex capital projects require much more than a simple spreadsheet comparing lump-sum quote numbers.

Bidders frequently appear attractive by hiding exclusions in fine print, utilizing inferior specification alternatives, proposing unworkable payment milestones, or excluding critical installation and freight costs. When contracts are awarded purely on the lowest face value, clients encounter immediate claims for extra variations, delayed manufacturing schedules, and zero commercial leverage once on-site work begins.

02 / WAKEFIELD SOLUTION

Disciplined Sourcing, Equalization & Delivery Oversight

Wakefield introduces institutional procurement controls that evaluate total cost of ownership, technical compliance, and commercial terms.

We equalize competing vendor quotations onto a standardized line-item basis, stripping out ambiguous exclusions and normalizing freight, taxes, and payment structures. We assist executive teams in fact-based negotiations, draft risk-mitigated contracts, and actively track fabrication milestones in factories through to guaranteed delivery on site.

04 / METHODOLOGY

How We Work: The Four-Phase Procurement Cycle

Phase 1: Packaging & RFQ Structuring Break the project bill into discrete procurement packages (Civil, MEP, Joinery, FF&E); define clear scopes, tender terms, and penalty clauses.
Phase 2: Bid Ingestion & Equalization Deconstruct submitted bids onto a uniform comparison matrix; adjust for omissions, disparate freight terms, taxes, and technical alternatives.
Phase 3: Commercial Comparison & Negotiation Present executive leadership with Commercial Comparison Statements (CCS) and lead structured negotiation rounds to secure optimal terms.
Phase 4: Order Commitment & Delivery Tracking Establish clear PO milestones, monitor vendor shop drawings and factory production schedules, and ensure synchronized site delivery.
