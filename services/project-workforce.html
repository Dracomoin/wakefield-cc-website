Project-based resource deployment according to role, location, duration and technical requirements. Managed within our integrated project control framework.

01 / CLIENT PROBLEM

The Challenge of Project-Specific Staffing

Capital projects frequently encounter sudden engineering resource deficits, short-term workload surges, or the need to supervise specialized works across distant geographical locations.

Direct permanent hiring is slow, commercially expensive, and creates severe severance liabilities once the facility reaches completion. Conversely, generic temporary staffing agencies provide unqualified resumes with zero technical vetting, no engineering oversight, and no accountability for on-site delivery performance.

02 / WAKEFIELD SOLUTION

Technically Screened, Project-Ready Deployment

Wakefield deploys qualified project engineers, site supervisors, quantity surveyors, and documentation specialists who operate under our established project control methodology.

We do not merely supply bodies; we deploy professionals screened by senior engineering managers. Our resources understand drawing coordination, IS measurement codes, daily progress logging, and contractor management, providing clients with immediate site control without long-term overhead.

04 / METHODOLOGY

How We Work: Four-Step Deployment Process

Stage 1: Requirement Matrix Formulation Deconstruct project scope, defining exact functional competencies, site location, timeline duration, and reporting structure.
Stage 2: Technical Vetting & Screening Senior Wakefield engineering leads conduct technical interviews, verify certifications, check past project references, and validate field skills.
Stage 3: Rapid On-Site Mobilization Deploy candidate to site with clear operational briefs, established DPR templates, and integration into client communication channels.
Stage 4: Performance Oversight & Demobilization Conduct periodic performance audits, provide technical backup from Wakefield headquarters, and execute structured closeout transitions.
