01 / CLIENT PROBLEM

The Risk of Unchecked Contractor Billing

Contractor billing frequently contains material divergences between tender BOQ quantities, submitted running bills, site measurement sheets, actual physical executions, unapproved variations, and supporting documentation.

Without rigorous, independent physical checks on site, project owners routinely pay for inflated quantities, unexecuted scope, incorrect architectural deductions (e.g. door and window openings in masonry or plaster), and speculative variation claims that compound across successive interim invoices.

02 / WAKEFIELD SOLUTION

Independent Physical & Documentary Verification

Wakefield operates as an independent commercial auditor standing directly between contractor billing submissions and client financial disbursement.

We do not audit from behind a desk. Our quantity surveyors go directly to site to take joint physical measurements (JMS), cross-reference executed work against structural and architectural drawings, analyze item rates against contract terms, and verify that every billed rupee represents verified physical output.

04 / METHODOLOGY

How We Work: Four Commercial Control Gates

Gate 1: Contract & Specification Baseline Deconstruct tender BOQs, technical specifications, measurement rules (IS codes/contract conditions), approved drawings, and agreed unit rates.
Gate 2: Physical On-Site Joint Measurement Execute physical site measurements alongside contractor engineers, record joint dimensional logs, and document site conditions with photographic proof.
Gate 3: Cross-Checking & Deduction Review Cross-verify mathematical extensions, apply mandatory structural deductions, isolate duplicate claims, and evaluate submitted variation rates.
Gate 4: Commercial Certification & Exception Report Issue an itemized reconciliation report with certified payment certificates, clearly stating verified vs. disallowed quantities.
05 / WHAT YOU RECEIVE
